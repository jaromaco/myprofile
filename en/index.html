What I do

  • Spatial ETL and automation

    For anyone repeating loads or joining sources by hand.

    Pipelines that download, transform, validate and load spatial data, in formats such as shapefile, GML, CAD, GeoJSON, GeoPackage and GeoParquet.

    Tools: FME, Python, SQL.

  • Spatial databases and cloud

    For anyone who needs a single reliable data source.

    PostGIS design and loading, with schemas, indexes and quality control, and data in S3 ready to query.

    Tools: PostGIS, AWS (S3, Lambda), GeoParquet.

  • Connected field data

    For anyone collecting data in the field and needing it clean in the system.

    QField and Survey123 projects connected to the database, with forms, validation and synchronisation.

    Tools: QField, QGIS, Survey123, Field Maps.

I also do cartography, web viewers and imagery and LiDAR processing when they are part of a data project.

Project

spain-flood-exposure: flood exposure in Valencia

A Python and AWS pipeline that downloads public data from the Cadastre and the INE, stores it in a data lake on S3 and converts it to GeoParquet to cross-reference buildings and population with flood zones.

Ingestion Lambdas with least-privilege permissions and tests · 266 municipalities · 1,830 census sections.

See spain-flood-exposure on GitHub

How I work

    Scope

    You tell me what data you have and what you need to deliver. We put it in writing: what is in, what is out, and the deadline.

  2. 02

    Execution

    I use the tools that fit your environment. I show you progress at the points we agree.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    You receive the data, and also the documented scripts or FME workspaces, so the process can be repeated without me.
